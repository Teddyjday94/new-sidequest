The process / 03

Six steps. One continuous conversation.

Every step remains visible and readable. The progression line is only an enhancement that shows where you are in the story.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Define the business, audience, current pain points, important content, and what a successful visit should lead to.

  2. 02

    Direction

    Set the visual tone, site structure, priorities, references, and what makes the experience feel specific to you.

  3. 03

    Design

    Shape the hierarchy, page layouts, content rhythm, interface system, and motion ideas around real content.

  4. 04

    Build

    Develop the site, wire up forms or integrations, add interaction, and make the responsive experience intentional.

  5. 05

    Revision

    Review the actual build, sharpen the details, swap content, fix awkward moments, and make sure it feels right.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Connect the domain and hosting, verify the important flows, handle the final production details, and go live.

FAQ / 04

The questions that usually come next.

What is a typical project timeline?

It depends on scope, content, and revision speed. A focused small-business build moves faster than a multi-page product or interactive site; I set expectations around the actual project instead of promising one timeline for everything.

How do revisions work?

We review the real site and make focused revisions to layout, copy, imagery, interactions, and responsive behavior so the finished result feels intentional rather than rushed.

Can you handle domains and hosting?

Yes. I can help connect the production domain, configure Vercel hosting, and work through DNS or handoff details when the site is ready to launch.

What about content and images?

I can build around content you already have, help organize rough material, and source or prepare supporting visuals where appropriate. Real business photography is especially valuable when it is available.

Can the site be updated later or maintained?

Yes. Future changes and maintenance can be handled as the business evolves, and the site can be structured so common updates stay straightforward.
