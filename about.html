About SideQuest / direct from the person building it

I’m Thomas. I build websites that feel like the business they belong to.

SideQuest Creative is my independent web design and development studio. I work directly with small and local businesses, service companies, creators, and product ideas that need a more custom web presence.

Design → Build → Launch

Why SideQuest exists / 01

Small businesses should not have to choose between generic and complicated.

I started SideQuest to give businesses another option: a site that is tailored to the actual business, built with modern interaction and responsive detail, and handled through one direct conversation instead of a handoff chain.

The goal is not to make every project loud. It is to make every project feel intentional — clear enough to use, distinct enough to remember, and practical enough to help the business move forward.

Capabilities / 02

The work between the idea and the live site.

I cover the pieces that let a project stay coherent from first visual direction through launch.

01

Visual design

Art direction, hierarchy, layout systems, typography, imagery, and the overall feel of the experience.

02

Frontend implementation

Turning the direction into a real responsive website with polished interaction and maintainable structure.

03

Responsive design

Making sure the experience feels considered on phones, tablets, laptops, and larger screens.

04

Deployment

Production setup, Vercel hosting, domain connections, and the final technical details around launch.

05

SEO foundations

Semantic structure, metadata, crawlable content, performance-minded delivery, and clean page organization.

06

Forms / business interactions

Inquiry forms, quote flows, booking paths, galleries, and other interactions that help visitors take action.
